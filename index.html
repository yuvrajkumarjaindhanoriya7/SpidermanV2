Titan Vorr
Powers (tap to close · press C to change class)

All classes: Hit · Jump x2 · Web-zip (pull to enemy) · Wave · Blast · Ult (needs full Power bar, fills by hitting)

Web Runner: Swing from buildings (hold Swing). Ult = Spider Storm hits every villain near you.

Shield Guardian: Hold Swing to BLOCK (20% damage, reflects bolts back). Blast = THROW THE SHIELD (free): it ricochets between up to 4 villains, hits harder each bounce, then flies back to your hand. You can throw again once it returns. Hit = strong bash. Zip = shield charge. Wave = Shield Slam (damages and STUNS villains for 1.5s). Ult = Rally Cry (+40 health, 8s damage boost). 3-hit combo: the 3rd punch is double damage and stuns. PARRY: tap Swing right as an attack lands to cancel it, stun the attacker and gain power. Shield throw hits harder with every bounce. Takes 35% less damage always.

Arc Knight: Hold Jump to FLY. Swing = Thruster Dash (smashes villains in your path). Zip = Rocket Strike (flies to target and explodes, hurting nearby villains). Blast = 3 repulsor bolts. Wave = missiles at 5 villains. Hit = wide palm blast. Ult = Unibeam through everything in front. Takes 20% less damage.

Special (Q / Special button, costs 30 power): Web Runner = Web Trap (snares nearby villains 2.5s). Shield Guardian = Shield Wall (almost no damage for 5s). Arc Knight = Drone Swarm (3 drones attack for 8s).

Controls: Pad: stick move, right stick camera, A jump, X hit, B zip, Y wave, RB blast, LB/LT swing/block, RT ult, Start class. Keys: WASD, arrows camera, Space, J, K, L, I, E, U, C, M map.
